# Adrenal insufficiency — implications bank

Condensed from NIDDK, Mayo Clinic, Endocrine Society, and Cleveland Clinic (retrieved 2026-08-22). Re-extract those pages before citing numbers in a new answer; this file is orientation, not a citation source.

## Types

- **Primary (Addison’s):** glands damaged. Autoimmune cause in 8–9/10 developed-country cases. Low cortisol; usually low aldosterone and androgens. Skin darkening, salt craving, dehydration/hypotension more typical. Crisis risk highest here.
- **Secondary:** pituitary ACTH low. Cortisol low; aldosterone usually intact (no fludrocortisone). No skin darkening; hypoglycemia more typical than shock-from-salt-loss.
- **Tertiary:** usually abrupt stop of long-course corticosteroids (or after cure of Cushing). CRH/ACTH suppressed; adrenals slow to restart (days–months).

Not “adrenal fatigue.” Endocrine Society: glands do not fail from everyday mental/physical stress.

## Scale

Addison’s ~100–140 / million (developed countries). Secondary ~150–280 / million. Peak 30–50 years; women more often for Addison’s.

## Everyday untreated picture

Slow onset: fatigue, weakness, anorexia, weight loss, abdominal pain, nausea/vomiting/diarrhea, postural dizziness, irritability/depression, joint pain, salt craving, hypoglycemia, menstrual/sexual changes. Easy to misdiagnose until crisis.

Physiology: healthy adrenals raise cortisol 2–3× under illness/injury/surgery. AI cannot.

## Crisis

Life-threatening. Low BP, low glucose, low Na, high K (arrhythmia risk). Triggers: infection, injury, surgery, vomiting that blocks oral steroid. Treat with IV corticosteroid + saline + dextrose. Pills not staying down = emergency.

## Living on replacement

Permanent primary AI: lifelong glucocorticoid (usually hydrocortisone 2–3×/day) ± fludrocortisone. Stress-dose for fever/illness/surgery/anesthesia/labor. Do not miss a day. Medical-alert ID + emergency injection kit. Extra salt in heat/exercise/diarrhea if mineralocorticoid-deficient.

Over-replace → weight gain, T2DM, hypertension. Under-replace → symptoms + crisis. Replacement doses are gap-fill, not anti-inflammatory megadoses.

Autoimmune Addison’s: screen for other autoimmune disease yearly.

Outlook: with correct replacement, full active life; life expectancy need not change solely because of Addison’s. No cure for primary disease.
