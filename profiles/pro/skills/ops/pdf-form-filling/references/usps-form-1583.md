## Page 2

Page 2 is mostly instructions and legal text. The only fillable area is the **Notary Public block** at the bottom. The notary fills this in if a seal is used — the applicant does not typically fill it.

## Placement Tips

- Text values: place at `(label_x0 + 5, label_y1 + 8)` with fontsize=9, fontname="helv"
- Checkboxes: place "X" at `(checkbox_x, checkbox_y + 6)` with fontsize=9
- The "n" character in extracted text IS the checkbox glyph (ZapfDingbats or similar)
- Column boundary is at x≈305 — left column fields are x<305, right column x>305
