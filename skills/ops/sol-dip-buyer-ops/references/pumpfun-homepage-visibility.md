# Pump.fun homepage visibility (knowledge bank)

Pump.fun does **not** publish a full ranking formula. Practical model from product surfaces + scanner behavior:

## Pre-grad (classic launch homepage)

| Surface | Dominant drivers |
|---------|------------------|
| Live / recently active feed | **Recency × buy flow** — SOL size + frequency of buys in last seconds/minutes; price velocity; TX count; unique buyers. Quiet grinds lose to bursts. |
| King of the Hill | **Bonding-curve mcap / progress lead** vs other live coins (small exclusive spotlight). |
| About to graduate | Proximity to **migration/graduation threshold** on the curve. |

**Helps click-through after listed, not listing itself:** ticker/name/image/socials, non-toxic dev tape.

**Does not alone rank homepage:** website, lore, holders with zero tape, single-wallet bundle that snipers flag.

**Hurts:** dump after markout, obvious wash, no unique-buyer growth.

## Post-grad (MARV-class)

After graduation/migration (e.g. PumpSwap pool):
- Leaves the classic **new-mint carnival** homepage race.
- Visibility shifts to Dexscreener trending/boosts, TG scanner bots, KOLs, Pump “graduated/search” UX.
- Report/cron ops (`market-direction-report`) measure **holders, liq, external pool growth** — not KoTH rank.

## Ops implication

If Cooper asks “how do we get back on the Pump homepage?” for a graduated coin: clarify **you don’t** via curve KoTH; you’re in post-grad discovery. Don’t thrash buy bots expecting homepage KoTH mechanics to return.
